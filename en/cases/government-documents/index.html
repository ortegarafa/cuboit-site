AI agents for city halls and governments: every request, in the right department.

A city hall or state government has dozens of departments, each with its own documents, systems and routines. A request rarely stays in one department: a contract renewal in Administration depends on Legal and Finance, a building project depends on Environment and Planning. When that link goes unnoticed, the case moves forward, comes back and loses weeks.

The problem

Scattered documents

Each department stores files its own way, in different systems and folders. Finding the right version of a decree, contract or case depends on who knows where it is.

Dependencies nobody sees

An Administration request that needs a Legal opinion moves on without it and comes back weeks later. With dozens of departments, no one can see every connection.

Pressure for transparency

Brazil’s Access to Information Law and oversight bodies require deadlines, records and a justification for every decision.

The solution: an orchestrator and specialist agents

Orchestrator

Receives every request, understands what it is about and decides which agents and departments must take part. It catches dependencies people would miss, such as an Administration request that needs a Legal opinion, and sends each part to the right place at the same time.

Specialist agents per department

Each department has an agent that knows its documents, rules and processes. It classifies, indexes and answers, always citing the source and respecting who can see what.

Staff research

Answers questions based on laws, decrees, ordinances and cases from any department, citing the source document, and drafts documents that a civil servant reviews and signs.

Citizen service

Answers questions on the website and WhatsApp about services, documents and case status. Every citizen request goes through the orchestrator to whoever needs to decide.

Transparency and security
  • Permissions by department and role
  • Every routing, query and decision logged, with an exportable audit trail
  • Traceable answers that support access-to-information requests
  • Citizens’ personal data handled under Brazil’s LGPD
  • Deployment on a sovereign cloud or in the agency’s own environment
  • Decisions always made by a responsible civil servant; the agents only assist
One request’s journey across departments
  1. Administration department

    Opens a request to renew a service provider’s contract.

  2. Orchestrator

    Notices the renewal requires a Legal opinion and a budget check from Finance.

  3. Legal and Finance agents

    Gather the contract, the applicable law and the budget allocation, with sources.

  4. Civil servants in charge

    Get everything ready in Cubo Flow and give their opinions in parallel, not one after another.

  5. Administration

    Decides with the complete case. Every step is logged for audit.

Why it works for city halls and governments

The orchestrator sees what no single person can keep track of: the connections between dozens of departments. Requests reach decision makers complete, opinions happen in parallel, leadership follows everything on one dashboard, and accountability gets simple because every step is recorded from the start.
