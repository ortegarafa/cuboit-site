FAQ

Common questions about AI agents.

What does Cuboit do?

Cuboit deploys and runs AI agents inside business processes. It offers its own platform (Cubo Core, Cubo Flow and Cubo Chat) plus implementation consulting, from assessment to managed operations.

How does Cuboit lower the cost of AI?

The platform uses an efficiency engine tuned to each client's business that resolves most conversations at a fraction of the cost of generic AI. Only unusual cases move up to a more advanced engine or to a person, and the cost of every conversation is shown on a dashboard.

How do you stop the AI from making things up?

Agents only answer from the company's own documents and always cite the source. Answer samples are audited continuously, and out-of-scope or low-confidence questions are handed to a person.

Do the agents work on WhatsApp?

Yes. With Cubo Chat, the same agent serves customers on WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, Slack and the company website with a shared history, and hands the conversation to a person when needed.

Can people step in on the agents' work?

Yes. In Cubo Flow, agents and people work on the same process board. You can set human approval rules by amount, risk or case type, and every step is logged for audit.

How long does it take to get agents into production?

The assessment takes about 2 weeks and the pilot on a real process takes 4 to 6 weeks, followed by optimization and expansion to new processes.
